# Code of Conduct

## 1. Be Respectful, Helpful and Kind
Offer support to others and share your knowledge in a friendly and constructive manner. Kindness goes a long way in 
making the project a welcoming space for everyone. When disagreements happen, approach discussions calmly
and avoid personal attacks.

## 2. Communicate Clearly and Politely
Keep communication clear, polite, and considerate, whether in code, code reviews, comments, or discussions.

## 3. Collaborate in Good Faith
Work together with others with the intent to create positive outcomes. Assume good intentions from others, and be open 
to feedback and improvement.

---

By participating in this project, you agree to uphold these principles.
